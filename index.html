WhatsApp Pay | Product Management Fintech / UPI

WhatsApp Pay - Why Growth Went Flat

500M users. ~1% UPI share. Here’s why.

  • Problem Diagnosis: Challenged the awareness narrative - identified zero marginal utility vs GPay/PhonePe and positive onboarding cost as core blockers behind ~1% UPI market share despite 500M+ Indian users
  • Three-Layer Blocker Model: Mapped funnel drop-offs across re-registration fatigue, recipient confidence gaps, and lack of conversational payment context using persona research (urban UPI user + kirana merchant)
  • Product Solutions: Prioritized UPI Bridge onboarding, in-chat recipient verification signals, and contextual payment triggers - RICE-scored and sequenced across a phased roadmap
  • User Flows & Metrics: Designed first-time setup, send-money-in-chat, and merchant QR flows with 6-month targets for onboarding completion (60%), Day-7 retention (55%), and chat-initiated transactions (35%)
